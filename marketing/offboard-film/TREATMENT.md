# Offboard — treatment

**Track:** Best Business Workflow, Flip the Switch. **Runtime:** 154.5 s (limit 180 s). 1920×1080, 30 fps.

## Direction: follow one grant

One GitHub organization membership — `oluwafemi-akolade` in `Optimus-pro-orgs` — is followed from the founder's motivation to the room request, the discovered permission, the human decision and the two checks that followed. A restrained lavender line (`#B4B6FF`) is the editing device: it draws Find → Remove → Verify in the opening, wipes each chapter, selects the decisive phrase in each capture, and joins the four recorded times in the verification summary. It is never presented as a product feature.

The shared Switch conversation and the human decision get the most screen time. Real captures carry the story; brand typography is used only where no footage exists (the founder's experience, the roadmap) or to summarise saved records, and is labelled as such.

## Honesty rules applied

- Every capture is real; none is recreated. Agent replies, approvals and provider results are shown only as captured.
- Each chapter names its run on screen: completed test, earlier controlled run, controlled run 14:23 UTC, separate hosted read-only check.
- Waits are not implied to be instant: the activity shot carries "worked for 57 s"; the scan, approval and checks show their recorded times.
- Internal IDs appear only where they are part of the genuine capture; no overlay or narration repeats them.
